Dargah History

Hazrat Sayyed Hafiz Allauddin Zia Chishti Rahmatullah Alaih, jinko log (Sakaldi Baba) ke naam se bhi jaante hain, ek buzurg Sufi saint the. Unki dargah Maharashtra ke Ambad taluka, Jalna district me maujood hai. Ye jagah Marathwada region ke mashhoor ruhani markazon me se ek hai. Hazrat Sayyed Allauddin Zia Chishti ne apni zindagi Allah ki ibadat, insaniyat ki khidmat aur logon ko sukoon aur hidayat dene me guzari. Mana jata hai ki wo door ke mulk Baghdad se Deccan region aaye aur yahan kai saalon tak ruhani taleem aur sufiyana paigham phailate rahe. Unki dargah aaj bhi Hindu aur Muslim dono communities ke liye ek barkat aur aman ki jagah maani jati hai. Yahan har saal Urs Mubarak manaya jata hai jisme log door-door se aate hain, mannat maangte hain aur langar aur qawwali ka intezam hota hai. Aaj bhi rozana kai log is muqaddas jagah par ziyarat ke liye aate hain aur apni zindagi ke mushkil waqt me dua aur sukoon talash karte hain. Dargah ka mahaul roohani sukoon aur mohabbat ka paigham deta hai.Unki dargah Ambad, Jalna me maujood hai

Karamat

Riwayat ke mutabiq Hazrat Hafiz Sayyed Alluddin Rahmatullah Alaih ko Allah Ta'ala ne bachpan se ruhani maqam ata farmaya. Isi wajah se unhe Hafiz ke laqab se jana jata hai.
